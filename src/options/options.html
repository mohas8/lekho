Lekho settings

Ridmik-style Bangla phonetic typing on any web page. Click the Lekho icon, or press the shortcut, to turn it on for a tab.

Typing

A list of dictionary words appears under the word you type. The Ridmik result is always first and chosen by default.

When you pick a different word from the list, it is chosen by default the next time you type the same English word. Stored only on this device.

Type 2026 to get ২০২৬.

Decimal points (১০.৫) and ellipses (...) are kept.

Privacy

Everything happens on your device. Lekho has no servers, sends nothing over the network, and only reads a page after you turn it on for that tab. Password and one-time-code fields are never touched.
