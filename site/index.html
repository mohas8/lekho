Why Lekho

The rules you already know

If you type Bangla on your phone with Ridmik, Lekho gives the same result on your computer: korrmo → কর্ম, TH → ৎ, qq → ঁ. Its engine is a port of the open-source Ridmik Parser, checked against the original on more than 20,000 inputs.

Types right in the page

No popup to copy from. Lekho writes into the text box you're using: plain fields and rich editors such as Gmail, Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp Web, X, Slack and Notion. Undo works as usual.

Suggestions that don't get in the way

A 150,000-word dictionary suggests spellings as you type. The Ridmik result is always first, so Space keeps what you typed; press ↓ to pick another word. Lekho can remember your picks.

Private by design

Works offline with no servers or tracking. It can only read a tab after you click its icon there, and it never touches password or one-time-code fields.

Install

  1. Download lekho-<version>.zip from the latest release and extract it to a folder you'll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions, brave://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder.
  4. On any page, click the Lekho icon or press Ctrl+Shift+Space. The badge shows বাং.

Help make it exact

If a word comes out differently from Ridmik on your phone, report it with what you typed, what Ridmik shows and what Lekho shows. Every report becomes a test case. Ideas and questions are welcome in Discussions.
